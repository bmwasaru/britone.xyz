title: "home-made anxiety"
date: 2026-08-21 01:04:25

4 hours of sleep  
irregular sleeping patterns  
2 cups of espresso, home-made  
5 hours of screen time, mobile  
notifications pushed, tick-tock   
football on the big screen  
unknown future beckons  
all home-made anxiety
